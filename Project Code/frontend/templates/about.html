About DeepGuard

DeepGuard is an advanced deepfake detection system developed as a final year project by a team of computer science students. Our mission is to combat the growing threat of synthetic media by providing accessible and reliable detection tools.

With the rapid advancement of AI-generated content, distinguishing between real and fake media has become increasingly challenging. DeepGuard leverages state-of-the-art machine learning models to analyze both images and audio for signs of manipulation.

Our Mission

We aim to:

  • Provide accurate and reliable deepfake detection
  • Educate the public about the dangers of synthetic media
  • Develop tools that are accessible to everyone
  • Stay ahead of evolving deepfake technologies
  • Promote digital literacy and media authenticity
