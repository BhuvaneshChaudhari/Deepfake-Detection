About Deepfake Detection

DeepGuard uses advanced AI models to detect potential deepfake content in both images and audio. Our system analyzes subtle patterns and artifacts that are typically invisible to the human eye.

Image Detection

Our image detection model examines pixel-level inconsistencies, facial landmarks, and other visual artifacts that are common in synthetically generated or manipulated images.

Audio Detection

The audio analysis system identifies unnatural speech patterns, frequency anomalies, and spectral inconsistencies that often appear in AI-generated or manipulated voice recordings.

Cybersecurity

As deepfake technology advances, identifying fake content becomes crucial for digital security. DeepGuard helps protect against misinformation and identity fraud.

Best Practices

For optimal detection, use clear images showing faces and high-quality audio recordings. The system works best with files under 5MB and processes 2 seconds of audio for analysis.
